## Implemented boundary

entire pinned producer reconstruction passes. Each non-null If outside While
ancestry has one exact positive and one exact complementary raw partition slot,
its original owner/list/block/chain path and branch objects, and predecessor
identity. Prefix descriptions retain their original command identity and polarity
instead of forgetting their owner. Inlined then/else, dedicated branches,
else-if predecessors and negative runoff all retain this owner. Callers cannot
mint a catalogue merely by finding a matching expression or wrapper declaration.
The fresh recheck repeats complete validation and compares every captured source
owner/path/branch/slot and raw block/command/transfer/target identity and order.

`InIfGuard` normalizes only the guard in a fresh scope tied to the catalogue's
positive raw assumption. It restores the previous scope in finally. Ordinary
positive and negative partition prefixes are still normalized through their
separate command scopes. A shared source Guard/call or raw positive command
does not share a target Application identity or invocation certificate. Missing
or foreign catalogues, including the unchanged direct-only constructor, supply
no new guard authority.

For a guard with direct eligible calls, a typed read-only semantic-field view is
captured before normalization and rechecked before substitution. It includes
constructors, child references, typed operator fields, type instantiations,
identifier/formal identities, literals, extract/concat fields, binder/let fields,
where expressions and expression-valued metadata. Exact constructor admission
rejects unknown constructors. Read-only referenced Body/DefinitionBody field
views are mutation checks only; they do not extend source-call indexing into a
Body, lambda or declaration. A field-view depth/cycle failure rejects the unit
without partial output, even when its occurrence expression is finite. This
conservative admission restriction is explicit and exercised in the source
controls. The one pinned TypeProxy backing field is read to avoid the public
getter's path compression; reflective structural equality is not used.

After the full original normalized tree and its wrappers are assembled and pass
owned bounds, `BindOriginalGuardOccurrences` records the owned Conditional's
exact statement path and every occurrence path of each captured Application in
its condition. Target copies inside that same condition are charged and bound
separately. A Conditional reused at another original path is rejected. Source
paths, original IR paths and per-mask replay receipts have separate roles.

Lower first rechecks the original F/N/A ownership/signature shape, complete
producer mapping, semantic fields and exact source-call paths. Unchanged
